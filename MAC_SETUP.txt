MAC QUICK START
===============

1. Open the restaurant_dashboard folder.
2. Double-click START_MAC.command
   OR open Terminal in this folder and run:
      ./start_mac_linux.sh
3. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000

Daily workflow:
1) Purchase / Expense Excel
2) Daybook Excel
3) Sales Excel
4) Sold Items Excel

No Tesseract, Homebrew OCR, or food-sales photos are required.

Recommended Sold Items columns:
Rank | Dish Name | QTY | Amount | % of Sales
